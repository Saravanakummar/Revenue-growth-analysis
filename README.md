1️⃣ Project Title

Revenue & Growth Command Center — SQL Business Analysis

2️⃣ Business Problem
Business Context

An e-commerce company observed increasing user traffic but slower revenue growth. 
Leadership suspects revenue leakage somewhere in the customer journey.

Objective:
Identify where revenue is lost between website visits, orders, and successful payments.

3️⃣ Dataset Overview
Dataset Structure

The project simulates an e-commerce analytics database consisting of four tables:

Users
Contains customer information including signup date, device type, and marketing channel.

Sessions
Tracks website visits, traffic source, pages viewed, and session duration.

Orders
Records customer purchase attempts and order values.

Payments
Captures payment outcomes including successful transactions, failures, and refunds.


4️⃣ Analysis Performed
SQL Analysis

The analysis focused on five areas:

Business Metrics
Total revenue, average order value, and revenue per user.

Conversion Funnel
Visitors → Orders → Successful Payments.

Revenue Segmentation
Revenue analysis by marketing channel and device type.

Traffic Quality Analysis
Conversion rates across traffic sources.

Customer Value Analysis
Identification of high-value customers and repeat buyers.



5️⃣ Key Findings

Key Insights

1. Organic traffic generated the highest revenue per user despite lower traffic volume.

2. Mobile users accounted for the majority of sessions but showed significantly lower conversion rates compared to desktop users.

3. A noticeable portion of orders failed at the payment stage, indicating potential checkout friction.

4. Revenue was concentrated among a small group of repeat customers, suggesting high customer value concentration.
